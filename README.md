# Toy Story 5 :movie_camera:

> When a tablet moves into Bonnie's room, the toys have to fight for her attention. 

**Toy Story 5** is the *fifth main film* in Pixar's Toy Story series, released in theaters in the United States on June 19, 2026.[^1] It is the 5^th^ movie in a franchise that began in 1995.

![Toy Story 5 poster](poster.jpg)

---

## Overview

Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Jessie and the rest of Bonnie's toys face a very modern problem: a frog-like tablet named ==Lilypad==. Bonnie receives it as a gift and becomes absorbed in it, so the toys' job of looking after her gets much harder. The story is about toys vs. technology, and it shows what playtime means in a world full of screens.
